Mapping Climate Diplomacy

Charting Africa's Climate Financing Agreements

This interactive map and its accompanying report illustrate bilateral and multilateral agreements made with African countries regarding climate financing projects. 

This visual mapping is part of APRI’s Track II Diplomacy project, which aims to identify ecosystems and strategic partnerships that can drive climate finance and green manufacturing investments in Africa. By mapping key actors, their strategies, and financial flows, this tool provides insights into the connections between African countries, China, GCC states (focusing on Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE), and the EU. Built on data collection from APRI and PIE partners, the visualization highlights opportunities for collaboration and more effective investment strategies to support sustainable development across the continent.

Please click on China, the GCC or the European Union to explore state-level cooperation on Africa's climate finance agreements. For more detailed insights, please select a non-African partner on the menu on the left.
